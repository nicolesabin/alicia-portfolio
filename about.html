About

Hey! I'm Alicia Nicole

I'm a graphic designer, illustrator, and hand-lettering artist who specializes in brand identity. I love to create strong, cohesive brand systems and the marketing collateral that implements them. I bring a sense of whimsy to my creations, so if you desire a touch of ✨ magic ✨ in your vision, I'm the designer for you.

About

Alicia Nicole is a graduate from Weber State University, having earned her BFA in graphic design with departmental honors in visual arts and graduated Magna Cum Laude. She was also the recipient of the Outstanding Graduate in Graphic Design award. She has 10 years of industry experience. She is a wife, mother, and lover of coffee and unicorns.

My Philosophy

I'll make sure your project looks good, but that is secondary to the strategy, message, and personality of your brand and/or collateral. My decisions are backed by strong principles of design and mindfulness of what you as the client want and need to convey.
