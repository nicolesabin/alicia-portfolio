Graphic designer, illustrator & hand-lettering artist

I consider myself a creative, dabbling in myriad forms of creative expression, stretching from cake pops to illustration, and all sorts in between. I landed on graphic design through a love of playing with type and the visual form of letters and words. My design solutions are often whimsical and playful, backed by strong hierarchy and structure. Inspiration strikes as fast as lightning or bubbles around me like a daydream, often leading to jumping up in the middle of the night to make a hazy, heavy-lidded sketch or thought before the idea escapes.
